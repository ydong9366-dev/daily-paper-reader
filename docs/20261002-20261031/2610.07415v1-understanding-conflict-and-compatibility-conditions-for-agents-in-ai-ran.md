## 摘要
解耦移动网络暴露开放接口，使独立智能体能够控制无线接入网（RAN）。目标不同的智能体之间的交互可能导致冲突。然而，现有冲突缓解方法将控制同一参数的智能体视为处于冲突中并需要对其动作进行仲裁，而未评估其目标能否同时得到满足。本文引入一种控制理论方法，用于确定智能体何时能够共存并共享对同一参数的控制。我们将文献中常见的基于阈值的智能体建模为采样死区反馈控制器，并通过关键性能指标（KPI）目标与容差来表示其目标，这些目标与容差定义了可接受的死区边界。我们从这些边界推导兼容条件，并表明任意数量智能体和被监测KPI的兼容性均可通过成对测试建立。我们提出一种冲突缓解策略，允许兼容的智能体共享对同一网络参数的控制，而仅在其目标无法同时满足的情况下进行仲裁。我们使用NIST的ns-3扩展ns3-oran进行仿真评估，结果表明：当智能体目标兼容且共享的可接受范围可达到时，该策略使智能体能够以接近零的服务水平协议（SLA）漂移共享控制；当目标不兼容时，其性能与优先级缓解（Priority Mitigation）相匹配。

## Abstract
Disaggregated mobile networks expose open interfaces for independent agents to control the Radio Access Network (RAN). The interactions between agents with contrasting objectives can result in conflicts. However, existing conflict mitigation methods treat agents that control the same parameter as being in a conflict that requires arbitration of their actions, without assessing whether their objectives can be satisfied simultaneously. In this paper, we introduce a control-theoretic approach to determine when agents can coexist and share control of the same parameters. We model threshold-based agents often encountered in the literature as sampled deadband feedback controllers and represent their objectives through Key Performance Indicator (KPI) targets and tolerances that define acceptable deadband bounds. We derive compatibility conditions from these bounds and show that compatibility for any number of agents and monitored KPIs can be established through pairwise tests. We propose a conflict mitigation policy that allows compatible agents to share control of the same network parameters while restricting arbitration to cases where their objectives cannot be satisfied simultaneously. We evaluate our policy through simulations using NIST's ns3-oran extension of ns-3, and our results show that it enables agents to share control with near-zero Service Level Agreement (SLA) drift when their objectives are compatible and the shared acceptable range is attainable, while matching Priority Mitigation when their objectives are incompatible.
