## 摘要
本文研究面向中继辅助的拓扑优化，以加速弱连通网络中分布式共识算法的收敛。不同于永久添加一组固定中继链路，我们引入一种分时框架，其中多种中继配置以概率方式被激活。一种基于ActiveSet的算法增量构建候选中继边集，并联合优化混合矩阵及关联的中继集激活概率，从而在优化过程中实现自适应中继选择。仿真结果表明，与固定基数中继选择策略相比，性能-成本权衡得到显著改善。

## Abstract
This paper focuses on relay-assisted topology optimization to accelerate the convergence of distributed consensus algorithms over weakly connected networks. Instead of permanently adding a fixed set of relay links, we introduce a time-sharing framework where multiple relay configurations are activated in a probabilistic manner. An ActiveSet-based algorithm incrementally constructs candidate relay edge sets and jointly optimizes the mixing matrices and the associated relay set activation probabilities, allowing adaptive relay selection in the optimization process. Simulation results demonstrate a substantially improved performance-cost trade-off compared with fixed-cardinality relay selection strategies.
